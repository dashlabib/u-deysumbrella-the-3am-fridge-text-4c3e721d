One line of 123 characters: the 3am text a smart fridge sends its owner — it has counted the fourth "just one bite" of the night, the leftovers have filed a formal complaint, and it is forwarding the complaint to the owner's future self. No milk, no ice, no door left open: the joke is the fridge as a weary witness with paperwork. The program validates the line against the bounty gate (1–140 characters, single line) and publishes it byte-for-byte to `/output/entry.txt`.
